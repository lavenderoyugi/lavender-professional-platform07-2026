## Overview

A personal Power BI project completed after my data analytics training to explore developer employment patterns, salaries, skills, work preferences and geographic distribution.

The dashboard was designed around practical business questions rather than simply displaying charts.

## Questions explored

- Where are developers located and how are employment patterns distributed?
- What work locations and job roles are represented in the dataset?
- How do reported salaries vary across countries?
- Which programming languages and technologies are most represented?
- How do education levels relate to employment status in the dataset?
